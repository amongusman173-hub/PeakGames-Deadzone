🌙 Day / Night

Night slowly spawns up to 30 zombies. New variants: Speedy (fast, fragile), Crawler (slow, silent), Tank (huge HP). Watch for Raiders — hostile survivors.

📦 Inventory

Grid-based inventory. Items take up space (e.g. water bottle = 4×1). Find Backpacks (S/M/L) to expand capacity. Crouch reduces zombie detection range.

📻 Communication

Proximity chat — only nearby players see your messages. Find a Radio Tower and craft a Walkie-Talkie (electronic parts + wire + CPU + battery) for long-range chat.
